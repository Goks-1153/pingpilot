Rules, in plain English

Rules run first and cost nothing. AI is only used, if at all, on chats a rule has already flagged.

  • No reply in 2 hours: someone asked something that needs a reply and no team reply has gone out → alert.
  • Pitch follow-up: a pitch was sent and not followed up within 3 hours, and again if still silent after 3 days.
  • Payment words: payment, invoice, PO, UTR or “paid” left unanswered for 30 minutes → Critical.
  • Commitment missed: a promised deadline passed and the deliverable has not been sent.
  • Quiet hours: no alerts from 20:00 to 09:30 IST, except a critical payment or a deadline due today.
  • Sunday off: no routine alerts on Sundays; critical items roll into the Monday morning digest.
  • Escalation: 2 hours → task owner, 4 hours → team lead, next working morning → your digest.

How alerts will reach you

  1. Now — this page. You review sample (later, live) alerts here and mark them reviewed. Nothing leaves this page.
  2. Next — a daily digest. A short morning / end-of-day summary generated from this same list, for your eyes first.
  3. Phase 2 — WhatsApp, only if you approve. Alerts to your number or an internal team group, sent only from a separate internal number — never from a client-facing supervised number.

Open decision: whether Phase-2 WhatsApp alerts are ever auto-sent for Critical items only, or always human-approved first. Default: OFF / always human-approved until you decide otherwise.
